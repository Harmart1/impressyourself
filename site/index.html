      Good questions

      Not sure if this is
      “an organizing thing”?

      If something repeatedly feels cluttered, confusing, hard to maintain, or harder than it needs to be, it’s worth a conversation.

      Is this only about decluttering?

      No. Physical organizing is one part of the work. impressyourself also helps bring order to digital files, inboxes, routines, paperwork, and repeatable work processes.

      Can small businesses use this too?

      Yes. Process mapping, clearer handoffs, simple procedures, checklists, and practical workflow design can reduce friction in everyday operations.

      Do I need a huge project?

      No. One room, one digital system, one recurring administrative task, or one workflow is a perfectly useful place to begin.

      Can Kelly help with downsizing or a parent’s home?

      Yes. The work can include sorting priorities, decision-friendly categories, donation and disposal planning, physical organization, paperwork, and a practical sequence for a move or long-term simplification. Start with the free Right-Size Roadmap above if you are not sure where to begin.

      Where is impressyourself based?

      impressyourself is based at 356 Queen Street East in Sault Ste. Marie and serves clients in the city and across Northern Ontario. Contact Kelly to talk about what kind of support fits your location.

      What happens to my booking details?

      Your name, email, selected service, meeting preference, and any notes are used to arrange the appointment. Public visitors never see private Google Calendar event names or client details; they only see whether a date or time is available.
