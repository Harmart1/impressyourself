## Do before public SEO launch
1. **Make the site public.** A private/login-gated preview cannot be indexed.
2. **Keep the production URL consistent.** The package is configured for `https://harmart1.github.io/impressyourself/`. If a custom domain is adopted, run `python tools/set-site-url.py https://your-domain.example` and commit the resulting URL updates before indexing.
3. **Confirm the postal code** for 356 Queen Street East and use the exact same address in the website and Google Business Profile.
4. **Set up and verify Google Business Profile.** Complete category, hours, service area, address (only if customers can visit), photos and services.
5. **Set up Google Search Console** for the final domain, submit `/sitemap.xml`, inspect the home page and request indexing after launch.
6. **Validate structured data** with Google Rich Results Test where supported and Schema.org validator for the `WebSite` / `Service` graph.
7. **Add original photography** when available: Kelly, organized spaces, digital/workflow work, and the Queen Street location if customer-facing. Use descriptive alt text that says what is actually in the photo.
8. **Earn local prominence naturally:** request genuine Google reviews after completed work; keep NAP consistent in reputable local directories; seek editorial/community links where relevant. Do not buy links.
9. **Measure search demand:** after Search Console accumulates data, use actual queries to decide what guides to write next rather than creating speculative city/keyword pages.

## Suggested Google Business Profile categories/services
Choose the closest real category available in Google Business Profile rather than inventing one. Describe services naturally, for example: home organizing, decluttering, downsizing support, digital organizing, paperwork/life-admin organizing, workflow/process design.
