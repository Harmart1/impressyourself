# Brand positioning

## Primary promise
**Organize the space. Optimize the system.**

Physical-space organization and process/system optimization are co-equal core services. Neither is positioned as an add-on to the other.

### Physical organization
Homes, offices, workspaces, storage, supplies, functional zones, downsizing and right-sizing.

### Systems optimization
Process mapping, workflow redesign, SOPs, checklists, digital information systems, administrative systems and implementation support.

The site should maintain equal visual prominence for these pillars in the hero, service hierarchy, booking flow and SEO.
